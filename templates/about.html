Vetia Searcy

Vetia Searcy is the Founder and Principal of ILS Corporate Services, LLC (est. 2005) and IVS Advisors, harnessing over 15 years of experience in corporate, finance, and real estate transactions. She holds a unique industry perspective, having worked on all three sides of New Markets Tax Credits (NMTC) transactions.

As a CDE Consultant, Ms. Searcy supervised the preparation of four successful NMTC award applications, closed over $267 million in NMTC transactions, managed compliance procedures, and sourced allocation for national and local CDEs. As Vice President of Community Development Tax Credits at JPMorgan Chase, she originated, underwrote, structured, and closed over $200 million in NMTC and hybrid NMTC/Historic and NMTC/LIHTC transactions, increasing closings in her coverage area by over 100%.

As a Project Consultant, she uses her expertise to guide CEOs, CFOs, and project managers through the NMTC closing process, specializing in sourcing allocation for rural, metropolitan, and underserved communities.

During New York State on PAUSE, she volunteered her expertise to advise underbanked, minority independent contractors and small businesses, connecting them with community-based banks that could assist with PPP loans.

Ms. Searcy is a graduate of Columbia College, Columbia University. She is an active participant in NMTC panel presentations across the United States. Notable awards include the Novogradac Non-Metro QLICI of the Year Award in 2017 and an Honorable Mention in the same category that year.
